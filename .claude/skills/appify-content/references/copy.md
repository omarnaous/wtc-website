## Outreach DM to a business (the version he approved)
Structure: specific compliment → the problem (his words) → the redesign offer → the money math → proof link →
question hook. Keep it short.
```
Hi <Name>! 👋

Your <products> are stunning, but we noticed your website has some bugs and feels very basic, not as
professional as your brand deserves. ✨

We'd love to redesign it into an elegant, custom website that feels as couture as your collection. Shopify adds
up to around $1,400 over 3 years. With us, you pay $360 once, with hosting included and $0/month after that.

Here's one we just launched: folliesdapresmidi.com

Would you be interested in skipping $1,400 in Shopify fees and getting a stunning new website? 🤍
```
- Research the store first (theme, product count, prices) and use real details; Shopify stores expose
  `/products.json` and the theme name in the HTML.
- Math must hold: Shopify Basic $29–39/mo → $1,044–1,404 / 3 years. Say "skip $1,400 in fees", not "save".
- Flag once if needed: card payments for high-ticket items, whether $360 template work fits a couture brand,
  claims like "bugs" he can't point to. Then send his version.
- Follow-up after 3–4 days: one friendly line offering the free homepage concept again.
