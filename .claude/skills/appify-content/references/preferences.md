# How Omar works (and what he likes)

## Who he is
Programmer and founder of **Appify**, a creative tech studio in Lebanon (websites, online stores, web apps, mobile
apps, motion graphics, Meta ads). Ambitious, fast, direct. Wants truth over comfort, but in task mode wants the
answer, not commentary.

## Response style
- Answer immediately. Code/copy/file first, one or two lines of explanation max.
- No preamble, no life-coaching, no "great question", no psychoanalysing how he works.
- Iterations are short commands: "make it shorter", "a bit shorter", "longer", "rephrase the first paragraph",
  "make it sound like ChatGPT talks". Just return the new version.
- When he dictates content, use his words. Flag a real risk once (one line), then do it his way.
- Swearing ("say what I fucking told you") = you added something he didn't ask for. Strip it back to his brief.

## What he loved (keep doing)
- **Magazine / editorial / premium** visuals: big serif or bold display type, real lookbook photos, cut-outs,
  colour fields, strips, punch-ins. "Cool and magazine professional."
- **Fast motion**: cuts on the 8th notes at 120 BPM, kinetic words, flashes, punch zooms.
- **Website showcases like the concept videos**: the live site in a browser window, one continuous scroll,
  chips naming features, then an end card with Dev holding a themed prop.
- **The mascot Dev** in every Appify piece: end cards, thumbnails, talking-head explainers.
- **A talking Dev who sounds human**: hooky, talkative, interactive, smooth with no stops, slower pace.
- **Precise, high-conversion messaging**: price in the hook, proof early, numbered steps, specific CTA.
- **Brand-consistent grid**: thumbnails and carousels in Appify colours so the profile looks like one system.
- **Real proof**: Darebni AI (live on the App Store), Follies d'Après-Midi (folliesdapresmidi.com), Appify's
  own reels.

## What he hated (never again)
- "Basic and very cheap" looking work (a plain lift/elevator film with flat type).
- Showing the website on every slide. Website goes in its own section / at the end.
- Old or fallback assets (the site's fallback desert video instead of the new hero film).
- Zooms/crops that hide the site; he wants it "in full desktop size".
- Sub-pages in a scroll when he asked for the homepage scroll ("full medium fast scroll of the website page").
- Text placed over faces / bad hook positioning.
- Content added that he didn't ask for (SEO points in an outreach DM).
- Robotic mascot voice or choppy delivery.

## Delivery habits
- Send finished files with SendUserFile; for photos send each image (not a zip) when he says "photos only".
- Always include: the caption ready to paste, a short timeline/slide table, honest flags.
- Reels: music version + NoMusic (for trending audio) + light encode + cover/thumbnail.
- Carousels: 1080×1350, numbered `Slide-1…N.jpg` (or MP4 loops), in order.
- Say what to change and where (file + constant) in one line.
